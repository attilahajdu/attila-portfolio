DRIP · AI WATER FOOTPRINT ESTIMATOR

How do you make
an invisible cost
understandable?

AI feels weightless. The infrastructure behind it isn’t. But a single “water per prompt” number hides more than it explains.

I built Drip to turn an everyday task into an estimate people can question, understand, and act on. The challenge: make the answer useful while keeping its uncertainty visible.

03 / UNKNOWN

Your actual water use.

The specific hardware, location, and cooling system behind your request. A modelled estimate cannot become a measurement by looking precise.

What this walkthrough uses

The image-generation path from Drip’s published v0.3 model. The provider is set to “I’m not sure”; upscaling is off. Text prompts are estimated at 1.2 per generated image, rounded to a whole prompt.

Repeating the same scenario produces the same result here: a fixed random seed makes comparisons easier to understand. This is a guided view of the existing model, not a fresh scientific validation.
